Sites and apps we've built

Questions before you start

Anything else, ask on the first call: (609) 289-7698

How does a project run?

A call about your business, a full design mockup we revise until it's right, the build, then launch on your domain.

Who will I work with?

Ben Kleva, who runs Shore Works. Before it, he built CRM tools for government technology resellers.

Do I own the site?

Yes. Your domain, your code and your content belong to you, with no lock-in.

Do you handle the domain and hosting?

Yes. We set up the domain, the hosting and the launch, and offer support plans after it.

What if I need changes after launch?

Send a message. Updates, edits and questions are part of the support plans.

Where do you work?

South Jersey, with restaurants, shops, contractors and service businesses.
