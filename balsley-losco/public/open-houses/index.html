How to make the most of an open house

  • Arrive with your pre-approval letter and a list of must-haves.
  • Ask about recent updates, taxes, flood zone and utility costs.
  • Take notes and photos, and note anything that needs inspection.
  • Ask the agent how long the home has been on the market.
FAQ

Common questions

Do I need an appointment for an open house?

No — open houses are drop-in. For a private showing, contact an agent.

Can I make an offer at an open house?

Yes. Your agent can help you prepare an offer after you've toured.
