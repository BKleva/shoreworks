First-Time Home Buyer Programs in New Jersey

By Balsley Losco Real Estate · Published 2026-09-09 · 6 min read

If you are buying your first home in New Jersey, there are several programs designed to lower the cost of getting started. Program details, income limits and purchase price limits change, so always confirm current terms with a lender or the program administrator before you make decisions.

NJHMFA programs

The New Jersey Housing and Mortgage Finance Agency (NJHMFA) offers first-time homebuyer mortgage products and down payment assistance for eligible buyers. Eligibility typically depends on income, purchase price and the property's location. Check njhousing.gov for the latest requirements.

FHA loans

FHA-insured loans allow lower down payments (as low as 3.5% with qualifying credit) and more flexible credit guidelines. They also carry mortgage insurance premiums.

VA loans

Eligible veterans, service members and surviving spouses may qualify for VA loans with no required down payment and no monthly mortgage insurance.

USDA loans

Some properties outside the denser parts of the county may qualify for USDA loans, which offer zero down payment for eligible borrowers and locations.

Conventional low-down-payment loans

Several lenders offer conventional programs with as little as 3% down for first-time buyers. These can be a good fit when you have strong credit.

Questions to ask your lender

  • What are the current income and price limits for the programs I qualify for?
  • How do these programs affect my interest rate and mortgage insurance?
  • Are there homebuyer education requirements?
  • Can down payment assistance be combined with other programs?
