Our story

Trust the name you know

Since 1954, Balsley Losco has been an important part of the growth of Atlantic County. We are solely owned and operated by individuals who have been local residents for generations.

Our goal is to provide service through our full-time, experienced real estate sales staff, ensuring quality and personalized service to every client.

Balsley Losco has been recognized among the top 10 independent real estate companies for sales in New Jersey and among the top 500 real estate companies in America — but our focus remains on you.
