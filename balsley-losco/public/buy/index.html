Buyer FAQ

How much house can I afford?

Lenders look at your income, debts, credit score, down payment and the property's taxes and insurance. A pre-approval gives you an exact number, and our mortgage calculator gives you a quick estimate.

Should I get pre-approved before I start looking?

Yes. Pre-approval shows sellers you are serious, speeds up your offer and helps you shop within your budget.

What is attorney review in New Jersey?

After both parties sign the contract, attorneys typically have a short window — commonly three business days — to review it and propose changes or cancel. It is a normal part of New Jersey transactions.

Do I need flood insurance?

If the home is in a FEMA Special Flood Hazard Area and has a federally backed loan, yes. Many buyers carry it even when it is not required. Ask for an elevation certificate and an insurance quote before you make an offer.

How long does it take to buy a home?

Searching can take weeks or months. From signed contract to closing, most transactions take about 30 to 60 days, depending on financing and inspections.
