Pre-approval FAQ

What's the difference between pre-qualification and pre-approval?

Pre-qualification is a quick estimate based on information you provide. Pre-approval involves a lender verifying your income, assets and credit, and gives you a letter sellers take seriously.

Does pre-approval affect my credit score?

A lender will typically check your credit, which can cause a small, temporary dip. Multiple mortgage inquiries within a short window are generally treated as a single inquiry.

How long is a pre-approval valid?

Letters commonly last 60 to 90 days. If you're still shopping after that, your lender can update it.

What documents will I need?

Typically recent pay stubs, W-2s or tax returns, bank and asset statements, and identification.
