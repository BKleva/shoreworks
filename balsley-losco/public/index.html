FAQ

Questions home buyers ask us

How do I search for homes for sale in Atlantic County, NJ?

Use the search on this page to filter by town, price, bedrooms and property type, or browse our guides to Northfield, Linwood, Somers Point, Margate, Ventnor and more. Create a free account to save favorites and get instant email alerts.

How much do I need to buy a home in New Jersey?

It depends on the price and the loan program. Many buyers put down between 3% and 20%, plus closing costs that are commonly estimated at 2%–5% of the price. Use our mortgage calculator and speak to a lender about the programs you qualify for.

Do I need a real estate attorney in New Jersey?

Most New Jersey transactions involve attorneys for both sides, and contracts typically include a short attorney review period after signing. Your agent can recommend local attorneys you can interview.

Does Balsley Losco help with rentals and commercial properties?

Yes. We list rentals through our rental search and application portal, and our commercial and investment division handles commercial properties.

How long has Balsley Losco been in business?

Balsley Losco Real Estate has served Atlantic County since 1954 and is owned and operated by local families who have lived in the area for generations.
