About Linwood

Living in Linwood, NJ 08221

Linwood is one of Atlantic County's most sought-after mainland communities, with established neighborhoods, mature trees and a mix of renovated classics, custom homes and bayside properties. It borders Somers Point and Northfield, so shopping, dining and the shore are all close.

Buyers often compare Linwood with nearby Margate and Northfield when they want a mainland address with a strong sense of community and quick access to the island beaches.

Best for: Move-up families Luxury buyers Downsizers

Why buyers choose Linwood

  • Linwood Country Club and nearby golf
  • Walkable pockets near Shore Road
  • Close to Somers Point's bay and restaurants
  • Mix of custom builds and renovated classics
