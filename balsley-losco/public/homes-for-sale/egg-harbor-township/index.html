About Egg Harbor Township

Living in Egg Harbor Township, NJ 08234

Egg Harbor Township (EHT) is Atlantic County's largest municipality by area and a popular choice for buyers who want space, newer construction and a range of price points. It includes communities such as Cardiff and Scullville and is home to Atlantic City International Airport and the FAA William J. Hughes Technical Center.

From established subdivisions to new developments and townhomes, EHT offers some of the widest selection on the mainland, with straightforward access to the Parkway and Expressway.

Best for: Growing families Newer construction Aviation and tech commuters

Why buyers choose Egg Harbor Township

  • Atlantic City International Airport
  • FAA William J. Hughes Technical Center
  • Large selection of newer subdivisions
  • Quick access to the Garden State Parkway
