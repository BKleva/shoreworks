About Ventnor City

Living in Ventnor City, NJ 08406

Ventnor City sits on Absecon Island between Atlantic City and Margate, with a boardwalk, a public fishing pier and a mix of single-family homes, duplexes and condominiums. Buyers choose Ventnor for beach lifestyle, walkability and a range of price points on the island.

Because most of Ventnor is in a mapped flood zone, buyers should review flood insurance and elevation details early. Our agents can walk you through that before you make an offer.

Ventnor City sits on Absecon Island between Atlantic City and Margate, with a boardwalk, a public fishing pier and a mix of single-family homes, duplexes and condominiums. Buyers choose Ventnor for beach lifestyle, walkability and a range of price points on the island.

How do I find homes for sale in Ventnor City, NJ?

Do I need flood insurance in Ventnor City?

Much of Ventnor City is within a mapped FEMA flood zone, so flood insurance is common and often required with a mortgage. Ask for the property's flood zone, elevation certificate and an insurance quote before you make an offer.
