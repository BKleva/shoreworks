About Galloway

Living in Galloway, NJ 08205

Galloway Township covers a large stretch of Atlantic County between the bay and the Pine Barrens. It is home to Stockton University, the Historic Village at Smithville and parts of the Edwin B. Forsythe National Wildlife Refuge, so buyers get open space alongside shopping and commuter access.

Housing includes everything from established neighborhoods to new construction, waterfront-adjacent properties near Oceanville and larger parcels.

Best for: Families University-affiliated buyers Nature lovers
