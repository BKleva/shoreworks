About Margate City

Living in Margate City, NJ 08402

Margate City is a quiet, residential barrier-island town best known for its wide beaches and Lucy the Elephant, a six-story landmark built in 1881. Homes range from classic shore cottages and duplexes to newly built custom residences.

Because Margate sits at the water's edge, flood zone and elevation questions matter. Our agents help buyers understand insurance and construction details before they commit.

Do I need flood insurance in Margate City?

Much of Margate City is within a mapped FEMA flood zone, so flood insurance is common and often required with a mortgage. Ask for the property's flood zone, elevation certificate and an insurance quote before you make an offer.
