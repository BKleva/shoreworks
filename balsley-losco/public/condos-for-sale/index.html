Condo and townhome living near the Jersey Shore

Condos and townhomes offer low-maintenance ownership with amenities like parking, elevators and association-maintained grounds. Be sure to review association fees, rules, flood-zone status and insurance requirements before you buy.

FAQ

Common questions

Are condos in Atlantic County a good investment?

Condos can be a strong fit for second homes and rentals, but costs vary. Review association fees, rental rules and reserves with your agent and attorney.

What are HOA fees?

Association fees cover shared expenses such as exterior maintenance, insurance on common areas, landscaping and amenities. Ask for the fee schedule and budget before you make an offer.
