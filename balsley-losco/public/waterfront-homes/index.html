Buying a waterfront home in New Jersey

Waterfront homes offer views, docks and beach access — and a few extra things to check. Review the property's flood zone, elevation certificate, bulkhead condition and insurance costs early. Read our guide to flood zones and flood insurance at the Jersey Shore.

FAQ

Common questions

Do waterfront homes require flood insurance?

Many do. If the property is in a FEMA Special Flood Hazard Area and has a federally backed loan, flood insurance is required. Get a quote before you make an offer.

What is an elevation certificate?

It documents a building's elevation relative to the base flood elevation and is used by insurers to price flood policies.
