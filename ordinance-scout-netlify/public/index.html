Results are by township. A township's result comes from the passages tagged to it: documents about that town, plus county documents that name it. Townships showing "No data yet" have no document of their own collected, so add that town's ordinance or master plan on the Sources tab or with Import. + means the ordinance welcomes billboards; ⚠ means it discourages them. Only actual ordinance text sets a firm result: wording found only in a master plan is shown as “leaning” because it is policy, not law. Keyword matches only; confirm with the municipal clerk, planning board, or a land-use attorney before advising a client.
