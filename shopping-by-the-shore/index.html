      Questions, answered

      Do you mark up the prices?

      Never. You pay in-store prices for your groceries, and my fee is separate and upfront — 23% of your groceries with a $50 minimum.

      Is there a membership fee?

      Nope. No memberships, no subscriptions — book only when you need me.

      Can you shop more than one store?

      Yes! Add as many stores as you like to a single order. I shop ACME, Aldi, ShopRite, Target, Lidl and more — and I'm happy to go anywhere else you ask.

      Will I get the same shopper every time?

      Yes — it's always me, Celeste. I learn your preferences, your brands, and exactly how you like your bananas.

      Where do you serve?

      Atlantic and Cape May County, New Jersey. Not sure if you're in range? Message me at 609.339.1600.

      What days are you available?

      I'm available Saturday through Thursday. Fridays are my day off — but pick any other day on the calendar and I'll confirm with you.

      What about the other services?

      Home & vacation check-ins, pet sitting, and plant watering are priced based on what you request. Send a booking request and I'll follow up with a quote. Additional fees may apply depending on travel, time, complexity, or special requests.
