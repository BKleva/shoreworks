From a secret to a staple

Dominic and Joanne Russo fell for pizza on their 2015 honeymoon in Italy. Dominic had already grown up making pies with his dad on Friday nights.

  1. Nov 2020

    Underground

    Square pies start coming out of the Russos' Egg Harbor Township kitchen. Only a handful a month, by reservation only. Word spreads faster than the pies can.

  2. 2021–24

    The waiting list

    New slots drop every Sunday night and Fridays and Saturdays go first. The secret pizza ring becomes a local obsession, and the Shore's papers and radio take notice.

  3. Thanksgiving 2024

    Legitimized

    With partners Eric Lemonides and Lee Felty, Squares & Fare opens at 7 East New Jersey Avenue in Somers Point, in a former laundromat turned corner pizzeria with a red door.

  4. Today

    Your neighborhood spot

    Slices are on the menu, there's a garden going in out back for fresh basil and herbs, and the same crew who opened the doors is still behind the counter.

Slow dough. Short list.

  • Dough fermented about 48 hours for an airy, focaccia-like crumb
  • Jersey tomatoes for both the red and the vodka sauce
  • California olive oil and artisan flours
  • Imported Parmigiano-Reggiano and in-house roasted garlic
  • Basil and herbs from the shop's own garden

Dominic's pie even made it into Pizzapedia by Jim Mumford.
