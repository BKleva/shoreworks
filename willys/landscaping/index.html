Built for Atlantic County

Yards in Atlantic County face conditions most landscape guides ignore: fast-draining sandy soil, wind and salt spray near Brigantine, Margate and Ventnor, and heavy clay pockets and high water tables further inland in Galloway, Hammonton and Egg Harbor Township. We select plants and prep soil for the conditions on your property, not a generic template.

FAQ

Landscaping questions

What are the best plants for a shore-town yard?

Near the coast, salt-tolerant shrubs, ornamental grasses and hardy perennials perform best. We'll walk your property and recommend plants that suit your exposure, soil and how much upkeep you want.

When is the best time to plant in South Jersey?

Spring and early fall are ideal for most trees, shrubs and lawns, when temperatures are mild and rainfall is more reliable. We can plan and schedule around the season.

Can you fix standing water in my yard?

In many cases, yes. Regrading, drainage swales and redirecting downspouts solve most yard-flooding problems. We'll look at the site and recommend the simplest fix.
