Built for Atlantic County

Sandy soil drains well but shifts, and Atlantic County's freeze-thaw winters and heavy coastal storms are hard on poorly built patios. That's why we focus on excavation depth, base compaction, drainage and edge restraint, the parts you can't see that decide whether a patio still looks great in ten years.

FAQ

Hardscaping questions

How long does a paver patio take to install?

Most residential patios take a few days to a couple of weeks depending on size, access and complexity. We'll give you a realistic timeline with your estimate.

Do pavers work in sandy South Jersey soil?

Yes, when the base is prepared correctly. Proper excavation, compacted stone base and edge restraint keep pavers level and stable.

Can you add a patio to an existing landscape?

Absolutely. We regularly tie new hardscapes into existing plantings, lawns and drainage, and we can redesign surrounding beds at the same time.
