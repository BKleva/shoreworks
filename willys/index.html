FAQ

Common questions

What areas do you serve?

Willy's serves homeowners throughout Atlantic County, NJ, including Egg Harbor Township, Galloway, Absecon, Northfield, Linwood, Somers Point, Margate, Ventnor, Brigantine, Hammonton and surrounding towns.

How do I get an estimate?

Submit the booking form on this page or call us. We'll set up a time to look at your property, talk through your goals and follow up with a clear estimate.

What's the difference between landscaping and hardscaping?

Landscaping covers the living parts of your yard: lawns, plants, trees and beds. Hardscaping covers the built parts: patios, walkways, retaining walls and fire pits. Most great yards use both, and we do both.

Do you offer ongoing garden maintenance?

Yes. We offer recurring garden maintenance as well as seasonal spring and fall clean-ups, for full-time residents and seasonal shore homeowners.

Do you work on both residential and shore properties?

Yes. We work with homeowners across Atlantic County, from mainland neighborhoods to barrier-island and bayfront properties, and we plan for the coastal conditions each one faces.
