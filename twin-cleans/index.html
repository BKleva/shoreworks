Our Story

Meet Jake & Dan Sinderbrand

Twin Cleans is owned and run by twin brothers Jake and Dan Sinderbrand. They grew up side by side, washing and detailing every car they could get their hands on, and turned that shared habit into a business built on one simple standard: leave every vehicle better than showroom condition.

When you book Twin Cleans, you’re getting both brothers and the same level of care on every job — whether it’s a daily driver, a weekend boat, or the family RV.

We come to you

No drop-off, no waiting room. Once your booking is confirmed, Jake and Dan arrive at your home, office, marina, or storage lot with their own water, power, and professional-grade equipment — and get to work right there in your driveway.
