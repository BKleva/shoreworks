Financing & warranties

Straightforward financing and service contracts, explained by people who aren’t trying to upsell you.

Financing

Competitive rates, worked out one customer at a time

We’ve developed very good relationships with the banks we’ve worked with over many years. Rates are competitive and based on the market, your credit history and the vehicle you choose. We work hard with each customer on a one-by-one basis toward the best approval possible for you.

Ways to apply

  • Stop by the showroom to submit a credit application in person
  • Call us at (609) 646-7676 to apply over the phone
  • Text or email a completed application to (609) 604-6057 or info@pumpkincars.com

For your security, please don’t send Social Security numbers through the website form. We’ll collect them by phone or in person.

Service contracts

Warranties built around your car

Most Pumpkin cars are sold with a minimum powertrain warranty of 1 month or 1,000 miles. We also offer service contracts that fit the needs of each customer and vehicle.

We’ve worked with Guardian Warranty Company for over 15 years, and our customers have nothing but great things to say about their plans.

We don’t pre-package a warranty into the price of the car. Every car needs a different plan, so we customize it at the time of sale and we’re always upfront about cost. The price of the vehicle is the price of the vehicle. No hidden costs.
