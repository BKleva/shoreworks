Meet Franck Freon

Racing champion, car guy and the heart of Pumpkin Fine Cars & Exotics.

The owner

Born in Paris, raced on three continents, now in Egg Harbor Township

Franck Freon was born and raised in Paris, France. He raced professionally for over 18 years all over the world, working his way from European single-seaters to Indy Car, then to the world of endurance racing, where he became a factory driver for General Motors.

That life taught him what a great car feels like, and what a bad one hides. It’s why Franck buys every car on the Pumpkin floor himself, and why he says he’s test-driven every one of the thousands he’s sold, autographing many of them.

Choose expertise, right pricing, pristine quality and service nobody else offers. That’s Franck’s promise, and his name is on it.

Endurance racing

Ten years at Le Mans. A Daytona 24 win.

Franck raced the legendary 24 Hours of Le Mans ten years in a row, then signed with the ultra-successful GM Corvette Racing team, where he won Petit Le Mans twice and the 24 Hours of Daytona in 2001.

Career highlights

From Formula Renault to the showroom floor

  1. 1986Racing begins

    Wins the Winfield Racing School Award and starts racing Formula Renault, Formula 3 and F3000 in Europe.

  2. 1991Indy Lights Rookie of the Year

    Moves to the United States and earns the Firestone Indy Lights Rookie of the Year title.

  3. 1992–93Two championship runner-up finishes

    Six wins, seven pole positions and 18 podiums in 36 races.

  4. 1994–95Indy Car (CART)

    Steps up to Indy Car and takes to Indianapolis Motor Speedway in 1995.

  5. 1996Le Mans class win

    Drives for the Mazdaspeed factory team and wins the LMP2 class at the 24 Hours of Le Mans.

  6. 1998Pumpkin is born

    Franck starts Pumpkin LLC with a simple goal: high-quality vehicles and genuine, personal care.

  7. 2000–03GM Corvette Racing

    Wins Petit Le Mans twice (2000, 2001), wins the 24 Hours of Daytona in 2001 and a GTS class win at the 12 Hours of Sebring. Podiums at Le Mans in 2000, 2001, 2002 and 2003.

  8. 2002Olympic torch

    Among the chosen few to carry the Olympic flame through the streets of Philadelphia ahead of the Salt Lake City Winter Games.
