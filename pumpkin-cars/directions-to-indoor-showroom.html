Directions to our indoor showroom

Minutes from Atlantic City, 40 minutes from Philadelphia, about 2 hours from New York City and 3 hours from Washington, D.C.

3084 English Creek Avenue, Egg Harbor Township, NJ 08234

Just off the Black Horse Pike

We’re conveniently located just off the Black Horse Pike (Route 40) on English Creek Avenue, between the Pike and West Jersey Avenue.

From Atlantic City (traveling east on the Pike)

Make a right on English Creek at the intersection with the English Creek Shopping Center (ShopRite).

From Philadelphia (traveling west on the Pike)

Make a left at the intersection just past Auto Lenders onto English Creek.

From the south (Cape May, Ocean City, Linwood, Northfield, Somers Point)

Take Garden State Parkway Exit 29 to U.S. 9 North toward Somers Point. After 3.3 miles, turn left onto Ocean Heights Avenue (CVS on your left, Wawa on your right). Continue 4.8 miles, and just past Mill Road turn right onto English Creek Avenue (County Rd 575). Drive 2.5 miles. Shortly after the traffic light at West Jersey Avenue (CVS on your left), we’re on your right.

Not a car lot. We’re an indoor showroom, warm in the winter, cool in the summer and always dry when it’s raining. Lost? Call us at (609) 646-7676.
